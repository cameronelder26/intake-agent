# Tailspin Health Alliance prior authorization policy

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT - INVENTED PAYER RULES - NOT REAL COVERAGE POLICY
Policy ID: THA-PA-2026. Effective 2026-03-01.

## Services that need prior authorization
- CPT 73721, MRI of a lower extremity joint: prior authorization required.
- CPT 43239, upper GI endoscopy with biopsy: prior authorization required.
- CPT 97161, physical therapy evaluation: prior authorization required after twelve visits in a plan year.
- CPT 77066, diagnostic mammogram: prior authorization required.

## Services that do not need prior authorization
- CPT 95806, home sleep apnea test: no prior authorization needed.
- CPT 99203 and 99204, new patient office visits: no prior authorization needed.

## Turnaround
- Standard review: decision within 7 calendar days.
- Expedited review: decision within 24 hours.
- Submit requests by fax to 555-0174.
