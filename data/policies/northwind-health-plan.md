# Northwind Health Plan prior authorization policy

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT - INVENTED PAYER RULES - NOT REAL COVERAGE POLICY
Policy ID: NWH-PA-2026. Effective 2026-01-01.

## Services that need prior authorization
- CPT 73721, MRI of a lower extremity joint: prior authorization required.
- CPT 27447, total knee arthroplasty: prior authorization required, with six weeks of documented conservative care.
- CPT 64483 and 64484, lumbar transforaminal epidural injection: prior authorization required, limit of four sessions per year.
- CPT 95810, attended in-lab sleep study: prior authorization required after a failed or non-diagnostic home sleep test.
- CPT 74177, CT abdomen and pelvis with contrast: prior authorization required.

## Services that do not need prior authorization
- CPT 45378, screening colonoscopy: no prior authorization needed.
- CPT 97161, physical therapy evaluation: no prior authorization needed for the first evaluation.
- CPT 99203 and 99204, new patient office visits: no prior authorization needed.

## Turnaround
- Standard review: decision within 14 calendar days.
- Expedited review: decision within 72 hours.
- Submit requests by fax to 555-0141 or through the provider portal.
