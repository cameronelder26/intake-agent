# Adatum Medical Plan prior authorization policy

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT - INVENTED PAYER RULES - NOT REAL COVERAGE POLICY
Policy ID: ADM-UM-2026. Effective 2026-01-01.

## Services that need prior authorization
- CPT 95810, attended in-lab sleep study: prior authorization required.
- CPT 64483 and 64484, lumbar transforaminal epidural injection: prior authorization required.
- CPT 93306, transthoracic echocardiogram: prior authorization required.

## Services that do not need prior authorization
- CPT 43239, upper GI endoscopy with biopsy: no prior authorization needed.
- CPT 74177, CT abdomen and pelvis with contrast: no prior authorization needed for emergency department orders.

## Turnaround
- Standard review: decision within 14 calendar days.
- Expedited review: decision within 72 hours.
- Submit requests by fax to 555-0185.
