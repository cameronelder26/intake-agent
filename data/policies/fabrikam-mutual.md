# Fabrikam Mutual prior authorization policy

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT - INVENTED PAYER RULES - NOT REAL COVERAGE POLICY
Policy ID: FBM-PRECERT-2026. Effective 2026-01-15.

## Services that need prior authorization
- CPT 27447, total knee arthroplasty: prior authorization required, with a BMI and imaging summary attached.
- CPT 74177, CT abdomen and pelvis with contrast: prior authorization required.
- CPT 95004 and 95024, allergy skin testing: prior authorization required when more than 80 tests are planned.
- CPT 92523, speech-language evaluation: prior authorization required.

## Services that do not need prior authorization
- CPT 45378, screening colonoscopy: no prior authorization needed.
- CPT 93306, transthoracic echocardiogram: no prior authorization needed.
- CPT 64483 and 64484, lumbar transforaminal epidural injection: no prior authorization needed for the first session.

## Turnaround
- Standard review: decision within 15 calendar days.
- Expedited review: decision within 72 hours.
- Submit requests by fax to 555-0163.
