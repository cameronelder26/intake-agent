# Contoso Care prior authorization policy

SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT - INVENTED PAYER RULES - NOT REAL COVERAGE POLICY
Policy ID: CCP-UM-2026. Effective 2026-02-01.

## Services that need prior authorization
- CPT 95810, attended in-lab sleep study: prior authorization required.
- CPT 95806, home sleep apnea test: prior authorization required.
- CPT 93306, transthoracic echocardiogram: prior authorization required for repeat studies within twelve months.
- CPT 43239, upper GI endoscopy with biopsy: prior authorization required.
- CPT 77066, diagnostic mammogram: prior authorization required only for members under age 30.

## Services that do not need prior authorization
- CPT 73721, MRI of a lower extremity joint: no prior authorization needed.
- CPT 92523, speech-language evaluation: no prior authorization needed.
- CPT 95004 and 95024, allergy skin testing: no prior authorization needed.

## Turnaround
- Standard review: decision within 10 calendar days.
- Expedited review: decision within 48 hours.
- Submit requests by fax to 555-0152.
